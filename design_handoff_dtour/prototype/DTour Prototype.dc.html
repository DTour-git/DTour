How to get a 9+ CGPA at DTU

A living guide maintained by students who've done it — without living in the library.

TNRM+12
Started by Tanya N. (COE '26) · 14 editors · 9 min read Checked by a senior · Oct 2026

1. How DTU grading works

Every course is relative-graded within its section. That means the class average matters more than the paper's difficulty — a 62 can be an A+ in a hard batch. The first thing to learn is how the grade boundaries are drawn, not how to score 90.

Internals (mid-sem, assignments, attendance) are typically 50% of the course. Most people who lose a 9 lose it here, not in the end-sem.

From a senior
"Ask your seniors for the professor's previous papers on day one. Same prof, same style, every year."
— @offer_letter_pls, IT '25

2. Mid-sems are 30%, use them

Treat the mid-sem as the real exam. It covers fewer units, it's easier to top, and it sets your standing before the end-sem curve…
